# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — 23 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summarising the world's news of Monday
23 November 2026, drawn procedurally with Pillow. `main.py` writes
`/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, deterministic, byte-identical on repeat runs).

Lead story: COP31 collapses in Antalya — no deal as the EU rejects a pact
that never mentions fossil fuels. Plus 15 more stories: the Hormuz war
widening, the Sparta II still burning, Kyiv, the US House, Nepal-India,
India's record 720, the Indonesian factory fire, and more. Every story
carries its source.
